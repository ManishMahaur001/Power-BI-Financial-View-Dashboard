# financial-view-Dashboard

Short Discription:

The Financial View is a Power BI dashboard designed to provide a
clear overview of a company's financial performance. It helps users
monitor sales, margins, profitability, costs, and year-over-year changes
through interactive visuals and filters.

Key Metrics

Net Sales -- Total net sales for the selected period.

Gross Margin % -- Percentage of sales remaining after cost of
goods sold.

Net Profit % -- Overall profitability after operating expenses
and other costs.

YOY Change -- Comparison of current performance with the
previous year.

Dashboard Features

Interactive Year and Quarter filters

Filters for Region, Market, Customer, and Segment/Category

Profit & Loss Statement showing major revenue, deduction, cost,
margin, and profit line items

Net Sales Performance Over Time trend analysis

Top/Bottom Product & Customer analysis

Regional and segment-level performance comparison

YTD and YTG analysis options

Year-over-year change analysis for financial KPIs

Main Financial Analysis

The dashboard presents key P&L components including: - Gross Sales -
Pre-Invoice Deductions - Net Invoice Sales - Post Discounts and Post
Deductions - Net Sales - Manufacturing Cost - Freight Cost - Other
Cost - Total COGS - Gross Margin - GM % - Operational Expense - Net
Profit - Net Profit %

Purpose

The dashboard is intended to support management reporting and business
decision-making by making financial trends and performance gaps easy
to identify. Users can drill into different regions, segments,
customers, products, and time periods to understand what is driving
sales and profitability.
